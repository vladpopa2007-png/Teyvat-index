Wish Rules & Guarantee Mechanics

160 Primogems / Wish
Character Event Wish (90 Hard / 74 Soft)
At pull 74+, 5★ rate ramps +6% per wish until 100% at pull 90. Losing a 50/50 guarantees your next 5★ is the featured character.
Capturing Radiance Streak Protection
Tracks consecutive 50/50 losses across Character Event Wishes: after 3 consecutive losses, Capturing Radiance guarantees the next 50/50 wins the featured 5★.
Weapon Event Wish (80 Hard / 63 Soft)
75% chance for either rate-up 5★ (or 100% if guaranteed from a standard loss). Epitomized Path caps at 1 Fate Point, guaranteeing your chosen weapon on the next 5★.
